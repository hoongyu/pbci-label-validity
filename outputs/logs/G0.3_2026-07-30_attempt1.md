## Result: **FAIL** — on the channel-interpolation KPI only

Every checklist item is met and the pipeline is sound end to end. The gate
fails on one number, and the cause is not a tuning error.

**`config.yaml`'s `bad_channel_sd: 2.0` and the ~0.34 channels/task KPI are
mutually inconsistent under any z-threshold reading.** On 62 channels, |z| > 2
flags ≈ 5% ≈ 3 channels *by construction*, regardless of data quality. This was
tested rather than assumed, over 24 recordings (sub-01…04, ses-S1):

| Threshold | log-variance | kurtosis |
|---|---|---|
| **2.0 SD** | **3.58** | **3.00** |
| 2.5 SD | 1.04 | 1.33 |
| 3.0 SD | 0.25 | 0.79 |
| 4.0 SD | 0.00 | 0.46 |
| 5.0 SD | 0.00 | 0.38 |

Changing the *statistic* does not fix it — kurtosis was tried specifically
because it is the classic EEGLAB channel-rejection measure and, unlike
log-variance, should not flag frontopolar channels merely for carrying blinks.
It still gives 3.00/task at 2 SD. Reaching 0.34 requires ~3.0 SD on
log-variance or ~5.0 SD on kurtosis.

The published "2 SD" therefore cannot mean "z-score across channels > 2". It
must denote a different kind of criterion — plausibly one relative to
neighbouring-channel correlation, or an EEGLAB `clean_rawdata` default. That
cannot be resolved from the references available.

**Not resolved by editing `config.yaml`.** The threshold is the user's
specification, and the same reasoning applied at G0.2 to the ECG pin. Tuning a
parameter until it reproduces a target number is also precisely the move that
would invalidate a reproduction attempt, so it is a decision to be made
deliberately and logged, not made silently here.

### Why this matters beyond the KPI

The channels being flagged are almost entirely frontopolar — Fp1, Fp2, AF3,
AF4, AF7, AF8, AFz, FT9. Those are not defective channels; they carry ocular
activity. Interpolating them **before** ICA removes the very signal ICLabel
needs to identify eye components. That may already be visible in the result:
only 3 components were rejected against a published ~7, and all three were eye
blinks. Over-interpolation and under-rejection are consistent with the same
root cause.

## Changed since last attempt
First attempt. Three defects were found and fixed while getting here, all by
running the pipeline rather than reading it:

1. `mne.add_reference_channels` leaves the restored Fpz at a NaN position. That
   NaN propagated into spherical-spline interpolation and into the topographies
   ICLabel classifies on. Fpz now receives its standard_1005 position, rescaled
   to this montage's own head radius (0.0973 m vs the template's 0.0883 m).
2. Bad-channel detection ran *after* the average reference; `gates.md` §G0.3
   orders it before. Reordered.
3. ICA was fit per *recording*. `analysis.md` §5 rule 1 requires it fit **per
   subject-session**, and the published ~7-components-per-session figure only
   makes sense that way. Restructured; bad-channel detection stays per
   recording because that KPI is per *task*.
