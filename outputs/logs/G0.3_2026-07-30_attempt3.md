## The mistake this attempt corrected

Attempt 2 rejected 1 component for sub-01 against a published ~7, and the
per-component distribution showed 6 more artefact components sitting in
[0.80, 0.90) — so that lowering the threshold to 0.80 would have yielded
exactly 7. There is even a principled-sounding argument available for it:
`GETTING-STARTED-claude-code.md` §1 notes the published pipeline used **MATLAB**
ICLabel while this one uses the **Python** port, and the two are not identically
calibrated, so 0.90 need not mean the same thing in both.

That reasoning was wrong, and it was wrong in a specific and instructive way:
**it was fitted to n = 1.** Across five sessions the configured 0.90 gives
4.8 ± 2.6, and sub-01 (1 component) is the low outlier of the five; sub-02
gives 9. The "0.80 → exactly 7" coincidence does not survive contact with a
second subject — at 0.80 the five-session mean is 8.6, further from 7 than
0.90's 4.8 is.

Had the threshold been changed on that evidence, the pipeline would have been
tuned away from the published value on the strength of one session, with a
plausible mechanistic story attached and a deviation-log entry making it look
considered. The between-subject SD of 2.6 components is the relevant fact: at
that spread, no single session can identify the threshold.

This is the second hypothesis of mine this gate has refuted. The first —
"over-interpolation starved ICLabel of ocular signal" — predicted rejection
would *rise* from 3 toward 7 once interpolation was fixed; it fell to 1.

## Standing caveats carried into G0.4

1. **`bad_channel_sd` was calibrated to the published rate** (kurtosis, 5.0 SD)
   because 2.0 SD provably cannot produce ~0.34 channels/task on 62 channels.
   Logged in `deviations.md` with its circularity stated. The red line recorded
   there stands: **re-tuning it after seeing a G0.4 accuracy is a new
   deviation** and must be logged as one.
2. **`iclabel_threshold` remains 0.90 as specified.** The MATLAB-vs-Python
   ICLabel difference is real and remains a live suspect for a G0.4 miss of
   1–2 pp, per `GETTING-STARTED-claude-code.md` §1 — but it is not to be
   adjusted pre-emptively on the evidence available.
3. **MATB yields 59 epochs per condition per session**, marginally under the
   "≥60 trials per class" figure attached to the 41.7% chance ceiling
   (`gates.md` §G0.4 step 7). Pooling three sessions clears it; confirm the
   published within-subject CV pooled sessions before relying on that.
4. **ICA is fit over the 6 in-scope recordings only**, not all 12 in a session
   (PVT, Flanker and 4 resting-state runs are excluded). `analysis.md` §5
   rule 1 requires per-subject-session independence, which this satisfies, but
   does not specify which recordings compose the fit. If G0.4 misses, widening
   the ICA fit to the full session is a candidate diagnostic — it would raise
   both the data volume and its diversity.
5. Fourth sign of a data-revision mismatch still open (absent TP9, 35.89 GB vs
   ~50 GB, archive nesting, "2 SD" vs 0.34/task). Mentor question outstanding.
